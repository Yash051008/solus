# Product Spec: B2B FMCG Distribution Management System (Nigeria)

Most important: the app must support both Push Orders (agents collect stock before selling) and Pull Orders (customers order first, then warehouses fulfil the order), with accurate inventory and payment tracking across both workflows.

## 1. User Management
- Separate logins for manufacturers, distributors, agents and retailers
- Role based access and permissions
- Customer and agent registration
- Customer KYC verification using BVN, NIN and geolocation
- Multiple branch management
- Territory and warehouse mapping

## 2. Product and Inventory Management
- Product catalogue with brands, SKUs, prices and quantities
- Carton to piece conversion
- Real time stock tracking
- Separate warehouse and agent inventory
- Low stock alerts
- Automatic restock suggestions
- Stock transfers between warehouses and agents

## 3. Order Management
- Primary orders from distributors to manufacturers
- Pull orders placed by customers or agents
- Push orders using agent carried stock
- Shopping cart and checkout
- Order tracking and status updates
- Automatic order routing to the nearest available warehouse
- Order batching, packing and delivery assignment
- Partial and missing delivery reporting

## 4. Agent Management
- Agent stock pickup requests
- Distributor approval of stock requests
- OTP verification during stock handover
- Geofenced customer visits
- Agent sales recording
- Automatic inventory updates after sales
- Agent performance tracking and leaderboards

## 5. Payments and Credit
- Digital wallet and virtual accounts
- Online and in app payments
- Split payments using wallet balance and credit
- Buy Now Pay Later (BNPL)
- OTP verification for credit transactions
- Payment history and transaction ledger
- Automatic payment reconciliation with ERP

## 6. Delivery and Logistics
- Shipment tracking
- Delivery assignment to agents
- Vehicle tracking
- Electronic proof of delivery
- Geotagged delivery photos
- Delivery confirmation
- Missing or damaged goods reporting
- Returnable packaging and empties tracking

## 7. Returns and Reconciliation
- Stock return requests
- OTP verification during reconciliation
- Tracking of stock picked up, sold and returned
- Open and closed shipment records
- Automatic stock reconciliation
- Financial reconciliation and audit trails

## 8. Dashboards and Reporting
- Sales and revenue dashboards
- Inventory and order reports
- Distributor performance tracking
- Agent visit and sales productivity tracking
- SKU performance analysis
- Financial reports
- Real time operational visibility

## 9. Loyalty and Marketing
- Distributor loyalty and rebate programs
- Retailer cashback and reward programs
- Coupons and promotional offers
- In app advertisements and banners
- QR code based product enquiries and ordering
- Customer surveys and feedback
- Shelf photography and merchandising verification

## 10. Notifications and Support
- Order status notifications
- Payment confirmations and reminders
- Stock and replenishment alerts
- Promotional notifications
- Customer support tickets
- In app issue and dispute reporting

## 11. Backend and Integrations
- Centralized database connecting all users
- Real time synchronization of inventory, orders and payments
- ERP and accounting system integration
- Automated ledger updates
- Complete transaction history and audit logs
- Secure authentication and access controls
